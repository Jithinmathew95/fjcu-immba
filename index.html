Introduction

學程簡介

An English-taught MBA with global mobility.

全英語 MBA，強化國際移動力。

Fu Jen’s MBA in International Management is AACSB accredited and designed to equip students with practical business knowledge from a global perspective. Only about 5% of business schools worldwide hold AACSB accreditation.

輔大國際經營管理碩士班通過 AACSB 認證，以全球視野培養實務管理能力。全球約僅 5% 商管學院取得該認證。

The programme was founded in 2006, when Fu Jen was selected with NTU and NCCU for the Ministry of Education professional MBA trial. Courses have been taught in English since the 2014–15 academic year.

學程於 2006 年與臺大、政大同步獲教育部遴選試辦商管專業學院。自 103 學年度起全面英語授課。

The curriculum connects marketing, operations, finance, human resources, technology, and strategy so graduates can work across borders.

課程涵蓋行銷、營運、財務、人力資源、科技與策略，並融入智慧營運與數位創新，強化學生跨國工作所需的整合能力。

Programme

Two pathways, one international classroom.

兩種學位路徑，同一個國際課堂。

FJCU

Two-year imMBA

兩年輔大 imMBA

Complete the MBA at Fu Jen. Minimum 42 credits, including 6 thesis credits. All courses in English.

於輔大完成 MBA。最低 42 學分（含論文 6 學分），課程全英語授課。

M1 + M2

Dual master’s

跨國雙碩士

Year one at FJCU, year two at UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS. Two degrees when both requirements are met.

第一年在輔大，第二年赴 UM-Flint、KEDGE 或 IQS。符合雙方規定即可取得兩個學位。

Exchange

Tuition-free exchange

免學費交換

Students not joining a dual degree may still study abroad through Fu Jen’s partner network.

未選擇雙聯學位者，仍可透過輔大姊妹校網絡出國交換。
