Curriculum

42 credits, including the thesis.

最低 42 學分，含論文。

The English-taught curriculum covers international business, marketing, human resources, global supply chain, finance, service design, digital innovation, and intelligent operations. Students typically plan three to four semesters.

全英語課程涵蓋國際企業、行銷、人力資源、全球供應鏈、財務、服務設計、數位創新與智慧營運。一般需規劃三至四個學期。

Undergraduate prerequisites

學士班基礎課程

Economics, Business Management, Statistics, and Accounting. Non-business majors must document one passed prerequisite before semester one, or complete a suitable undergraduate course at FJCU. Prerequisite credits do not count toward graduation.

經濟學、管理學（企業管理）、統計學、會計學。非商管主修須於開學前繳交其中一科合格證明，否則第一學期於大學部補修；補修不列入畢業學分。

Postgraduate waivers

碩士班學分抵免

Students who previously completed imMBA professional courses may apply once, in the first week of the entering semester, subject to the chair’s approval.

曾修習本碩士班專業課程且符合資格者，入學當學期開學一週內向註冊組提出，以一次為限，並經主任核定。
