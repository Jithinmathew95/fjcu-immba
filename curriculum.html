Fu Jen imMBA is an AACSB-accredited, English-taught master’s programme. The curriculum covers international business, marketing, human resources, global supply chain, finance, and service design, together with skills for multinational operations, smart operations, and digital innovation. Students typically plan three to four semesters.

輔大國際經管（imMBA）為 AACSB 認證的全英碩士班。課程涵蓋國際企業管理、行銷、人力資源、全球供應鏈管理、財務管理、服務設計，並融入跨國企業經營所需技能與智慧營運、數位創新。一般需規劃 3–4 個學期。

Course waiver (undergraduate level)

入學前學士班基礎課程

Economics, Business Management, Statistics, and Accounting are prerequisite courses. Non-business majors must submit an official transcript proving one passed prerequisite before semester one, or complete a suitable undergraduate course at FJCU. Contact the office first. Prerequisite credits do not count toward graduation.

設經濟學、管理學（企業管理）、統計學、會計學為基礎課程。大學非商管主修須於第一學期開學前繳交其中一科合格證明，否則第一學期於輔大學士班補修一科。補修前請向所辦確認課程名稱與學分；補修不列入畢業學分。

Course waiver (postgraduate level)

入學前碩士班學分抵免

Students who previously completed imMBA professional courses may apply once, in the first week of the entering semester, subject to the chair’s approval.

凡曾於本碩士班修習專業課程且符合資格者，得於入學當學期向註冊組提出，以一次為限，開學後一週截止。請備成績單正本、抵認科目申請表及修習科目學分證明。

Graduation requirements

修業規則

Minimum graduation credits are 42, including 6 thesis credits. Rules cover prerequisites (non-business majors), required and elective professional courses, the thesis, and the English graduation threshold. Students enrolled from Fall 2025 and from Fall 2024 should follow the version that applies to their intake.

最低畢業學分為 42 學分（含論文 6 學分）。基礎課程、專業必修、專業選修、論文撰寫、英文畢業門檻皆有規定。Fall 2025 入學起適用最新修業規則。請詳讀並配合辦理，以順利取得輔大 MBA 學位。
