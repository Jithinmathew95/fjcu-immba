Overseas exchange

Go abroad without a dual degree.

不必雙聯，也能出國。

Students who do not join UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS may still use Fu Jen’s tuition-free exchange network through the International Student Center. This is the standard way to add a semester abroad while remaining in the two-year FJCU imMBA.

未選擇 UM-Flint、KEDGE 或 IQS 雙聯者，仍可透過輔大國際學生中心的免學費交換網絡出國一學期，並繼續完成兩年輔大 imMBA。

Exchange and dual degree are different tracks. Dual degree requires a week-one declaration and extra partner tuition in some cases. Exchange uses university-wide partner seats. 交換與雙聯為不同路徑。雙聯須於第一週提出意向，部分合作校另收學費；交換則使用全校姊妹校名額。
