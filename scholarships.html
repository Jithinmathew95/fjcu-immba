Tuition reductions and awards.

學雜費減免與獎助學金。

The standard tuition and miscellaneous fees for international students are NT$123,000 per semester. Students may receive only one of the four awards below.

1–3. Government and FJCU new-student scholarships

  • MOE Taiwan Scholarship
  • MOFA Taiwan Scholarship
  • FJCU Scholarship for New International Students in Master’s Degree Programmes

Recipients of scholarships 1–3 also receive an imMBA tuition reduction from the international rate to the local rate: NT$123,000 → NT$68,660 per semester.

MOE and MOFA recipients keep the local rate while the government scholarship is active, for up to two years. FJCU Scholarship recipients keep the local rate for up to four consecutive semesters. A leave of absence ends the imMBA reduction.

4. FJCU President’s Scholarship

New imMBA international students from an English-speaking country, or with TOEFL iBT 72 / IELTS 6 / TOEIC 785 (CEFR B2) or above, receive NT$40,600 off per semester for up to four consecutive semesters: NT$123,000 → NT$82,400.

No separate application is required; the reduction appears on the tuition slip. Students must enrol in the admitted semester and continue without interruption.
