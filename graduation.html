Graduation

42 credits, including the thesis.

最低 42 學分，含論文。

Read the graduation rules for your entering year. Typical completion takes three to four semesters. Dual-degree students must also meet the partner school’s requirements before applying for the Fu Jen thesis defence.

請依入學年度閱讀修業規則。一般需三至四個學期。雙聯學生須同時完成合作校規定，始得申請輔大論文口試。

  • Advisor lists: late March and early October / September.
  • 指導教授名單於 3 月底與 9–10 月初公布。
  • Enrol in THESIS before add/drop in the semester you plan to defend.
  • 擬口試當學期須於加退選前加選「論文」。
