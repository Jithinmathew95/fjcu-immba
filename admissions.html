International students

All courses are in English. Choose the two-year FJCU imMBA or a dual master’s: M1 at FJCU and M2 at UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS. Partner schools may charge extra tuition.

Spring 2027 is open now.
Application period: 14 September – 14 October.
Typical spring window: mid-October to late December.
Typical fall window: January to June, often in two rounds.

Apply online

Required documents

  • Autobiography (English)
  • Study plan (English)
  • English proficiency certificate (TOEFL, IELTS, or TOEIC; CEFR B2 preferred)
  • Two recommendation letters
  • Other supporting materials

The full system also requests diploma, transcripts, financial statement, application-fee receipt, nationality verification, and a declaration form. Confirm the current list in the brochure.

Tuition and scholarships

International tuition and miscellaneous fees: NT$123,000 per semester.

Preferential plan: students with ROC government scholarships or FJCU scholarships for new international master’s students pay local-equivalent fees of NT$68,660 per semester.

President Scholarship: TOEFL iBT, IELTS, or TOEIC at CEFR B2 or above (valid within two years), or nationals of English-speaking countries, receive NT$40,600 per semester for up to four semesters.

本地生招生

國際經管每年設甄試與碩士兩次招生，皆免筆試，適合不同主修、希望強化國際移動力的同學。審查含書審與口試（英文為主、中文為輔）。

甄試：約每年 10 月報名、11 月口試、12 月放榜；2 月或 9 月入學。已取得學士學位者可提前於 2 月入學。

碩士班：約每年 1 月報名、3 月口試並放榜；9 月入學。

116 甄試報名：2026/09/30–10/13（請以簡章為準）。
