Dual master’s

One year at Fu Jen, one year abroad.

一年輔大，一年海外。

Students may complete two master’s degrees in two years if both institutions’ graduation requirements are met. Declare interest in week one of the first semester.

若符合雙方畢業規定，兩年可取得兩個碩士學位。須於第一學期第一週向辦公室提出意向。

Study schedule

修業時程

M1 Fall

Submit the dual-degree application in week one, follow the partner contract course plan, and confirm a thesis advisor.

第一週送出雙聯申請、依合約修課，並確認論文指導教授。

M1 Spring

Complete the first three-chapter presentation, English-proficiency documents, thesis defence agreement, and optional imMBA scholarship application. Apply to the partner school directly; Fu Jen recommends within seat limits.

完成論文前三章發表與相關文件，並向合作學校提出申請；名額有限，由學程依表現推薦。

M2 Fall

Register and pay FJCU tuition, take courses at the partner school, and send the registered course list to Fu Jen.

完成輔大註冊繳費，於合作校修課，並繳交選課清單。

M2 Spring

Finish partner requirements (including internships where required) and complete the Fu Jen thesis defence.

完成合作校課程（含實習要求）與輔大論文口試及畢業程序。

Missing the schedule means withdrawing from that dual-degree cycle. Reapplication starts from phase 1. If qualified applicants exceed contract seats, priority is junior standing, academic performance, then English proficiency.

未依時程辦理視同放棄該次雙聯申請，須自第一階段重新提出。名額不足時，優先序為一年級、學業表現、英語能力。

USA

University of Michigan–Flint

AACSB MBA with multiple concentrations. First-year FJCU requirements include the contract course plan, GPA 3.0, and TOEFL iBT. UM-Flint offers in-state tuition and scholarships to imMBA students.

AACSB MBA，多種專長。輔大第一年須完成合約課程、GPA 3.0 與 TOEFL iBT。UM-Flint 提供州內學費與獎學金。

France

KEDGE Business School

Triple crown (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS). Master in Management (60 ECTS) plus at least six months of internship or practical experience. Students need 240 ECTS (120 credits) from undergraduate study. Study at Bordeaux or Marseille; KEDGE tuition is covered while paying full FJCU fees.

AACSB、AMBA、EQUIS 三認證。Master in Management（60 ECTS）並需至少六個月實習或實務經驗。大學須具備 240 ECTS（120 學分）。可選 Bordeaux 或 Marseille；繳交輔大學費即可就讀。

Spain

IQS School of Management

AACSB school in Barcelona. Dual options include Master in International Marketing in a Digital Environment (MIMDE) and Master in Industrial Business Management (MIBM). GPA and English requirements apply; tuition is reviewed annually.

位於巴塞隆納，AACSB 認證。可選國際數位行銷碩士（MIMDE）或工業企業管理碩士（MIBM）。另有 GPA 與英語門檻，學費每年檢討。
