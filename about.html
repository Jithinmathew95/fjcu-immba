Practical management, global mobility.

實務管理，國際移動。

Fu Jen’s MBA in International Management is AACSB accredited and designed to equip students with practical business knowledge from a global perspective. Only about 5% of business schools worldwide hold AACSB accreditation.

輔大國際經營管理碩士班通過 AACSB 認證，以全球視野培養實務管理能力。全球約僅 5% 商管學院取得該認證。

The curriculum connects marketing, operations, finance, human resources, technology, and strategy so graduates can work across borders.

課程涵蓋行銷、營運、財務、人力資源、科技與策略，強化學生跨國工作所需的整合能力。

International classroom

國際化課堂

Degree-seeking and exchange students come from more than 30 countries. The local to non-local ratio in class can be 1:1.

學位生與交換生來自三十餘國，課堂本地生與非本地生比例可達 1:1。

Dual degrees

雙聯學位

Partner options with UM-Flint (USA), KEDGE (France), and IQS (Spain) let students pursue two master’s degrees in two years.

與美國 UM-Flint、法國 KEDGE、西班牙 IQS 合作，兩年可攻讀兩個碩士學位。

English throughout

全英語授課

Courses are conducted in English. The programme has been a flagship international offering of the College of Management since its professional MBA trial with the Ministry of Education in 2006.
