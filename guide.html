How to study in the programme.

如何在國際經管就讀。

Browse this site about one month before the semester starts. The office emails course-selection details about three weeks before classes begin. Follow that message closely.

開學前一個月請瀏覽本站了解修課與畢業規定。所辦約於開學前三週以 Email 公告選課細節，請依信件內容辦理。

First week

入學第一週

  • Declare dual-degree interest to the office if you plan UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS.
  • 若計畫雙聯學位，須於第一週向辦公室提出意向。
  • Non-business majors: submit proof of Economics, Management, Statistics, or Accounting — or arrange a prerequisite.
  • 非商管主修請繳交經濟學、管理學、統計學或會計學證明，或辦理補修。
  • Course waivers at postgraduate level may be requested only once, in the first week.
  • 碩士班學分抵免僅能於開學一週內申請一次。
